# Onboarding and ongoing workflow

Run `python3 scripts/setup.py --resume /path/to/resume.pdf`, or omit the flag and place the file under `workspace/resume/`. Setup preserves existing data. Use the agent's available document tools to read the resume. Ask for text if it cannot be read. Keep the original as evidence.

Start from resume facts and ask for the few things you cannot infer: role families, regions and remote/on-site preferences, when the candidate can work, graduation and degree level if relevant, sponsorship or work authorization if they want to disclose it, compensation, and other limits. Do not ask for birthdates, account credentials, or identity numbers. Ask whether full-time academic-term work is possible before assuming a conflict. Save screening preferences in `profile.json` and factual background in `profile.md`. Record explicitly confirmed reusable form facts in `application-answers.md`.

For each past role or project, capture official title, dates, responsibilities, what the candidate personally delivered, methods, and supported results. Use consistent tags in `tags-taxonomy.md` and mark uncertain or disputed facts `NEEDS CONFIRMATION`. Keep resume-ready bullets separate from raw interview notes. Ask for plausible missing metrics the candidate can substantiate. Record scope, ownership and uncertainty along with each figure.

On a regular search: discover, confirm, screen, check duplicates, prioritize, prepare packages, then reconcile statuses and surface actionable work. Give a short account of what was added, deadlines or unresolved checks, and which sources could not be read. Preserve an evidence trail across sessions.
