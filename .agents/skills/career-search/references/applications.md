# Applications and networking

## Application prep and reconciliation

Read the actual form. Match education, dates, location, links and work authorization to the candidate's confirmed answers. Never infer demographic answers or how the candidate wants to respond to voluntary prompts. Draft before filling anything that saves remotely. Verify the employer, requisition, location, resume and answers immediately before an authorized submission. Stop when a new candidate choice or authentication prompt is required. Record the exact confirmed outcome. If unclear, mark for verification.

For Greenhouse and Lever, distinguish the employer listing from a redirect or apply page. On Ashby, verify the job board slug and requisition before staging answers. For Workday, inspect what resume parsing filled, especially line breaks, institution names, dates and role descriptions. For other platforms, follow their visible requirements. Record log-in or account barriers so the user can handle them. Open the native posting link for actions.

Answer “why this role or company” with specific aspects of the team, product, problem or day-to-day work from the posting, plus why they appeal. Answer project prompts with relevant evidence from the specific project. Skip claims the candidate cannot stand behind.

Reconcile inbox or portal evidence only through the user's approved connection or export. Match by requisition ID, job link, specific title and applicant timeline. Read the relevant thread or portal state. If a company-only receipt could apply to more than one job, hold the update for verification. Keep dates for applications, screens, assessments, interviews, offers, rejections, and withdrawal. Show deadlines and next actions in the dashboard.

## Referral desk and outreach

Maintain company-level coverage while keeping connection, conversation, meeting, referral and referral mechanism as independent facts. Verify a person actually works on a relevant team before calling them a referral path. For a code or employee-initiated application, consider asking first if rapport exists. If a referral can be attached later or the role is time-sensitive, proceed after user approval and pursue the handoff separately. Never delay a time-sensitive application indefinitely while waiting for a speculative introduction.

Draft one specific, easy-to-answer question about the person's team or experience. Make referral requests for specific requisitions when there is credible rapport. An unanswered invitation or an accepted connection alone does not make a contact ready for a referral request. Respect the user's chosen follow-up cap and cadence. A good starting point is at least seven days between follow-ups and at most two to an unanswered cold introduction. Stop on an opt-out, platform warning or ambiguous send result. Treat the dashboard inbox as a queue for user review. Monitor replies and deadlines without sending on the user's behalf unless authorized for that action.
